Mission Critical · Energy Services & Resiliency

Spend less to run the building. Stay running when it counts.

Energy services cut what your portfolio pays for every kilowatt-hour. Resiliency makes sure the facility keeps operating through a heat wave, a storm, or a grid event. FirstCall delivers both — engineered, self-performed, and measured across every site.

Energy services

Waste hides in the mechanical plant. We know where to look.

Aging chillers, drifting controls, simultaneous heating and cooling, lighting that never turns off — across dozens or hundreds of sites, small inefficiencies become a line item nobody can explain. We benchmark how each building actually uses energy, prioritize the measures with the fastest payback, and self-perform the work.

For larger programs, performance contracting lets the savings fund the upgrade, with results measured against a documented baseline.

  • Investment-grade auditsSite-by-site benchmarking that turns utility bills into a prioritized measure list.
  • Retro-commissioningTune air and water systems back to design intent — often the fastest payback in the building.
  • Controls & EMS optimizationSchedules, setpoints, and sequences managed centrally across the portfolio.
  • Measured & verifiedSavings tracked against a baseline so results are documented, not promised.
Measures we deliver

Where the savings come from.

Chiller, boiler & RTU modernization

Replace and right-size equipment that's past its efficient life — planned as capital, not as an emergency.

Building automation & controls

BAS/BMS upgrades and sequences that stop equipment from fighting itself.

HVAC optimization & retrocommissioning

Economizers, VFDs, static-pressure resets, and airflow tuned to real occupancy.

LED retrofits & lighting controls

Turnkey LED conversions with occupancy and daylight controls — and the rebate paperwork handled.

Utility rebates & incentives

Metering, monitoring & analytics

Portfolio-level visibility that turns one-time savings into a permanent baseline.

Resiliency

Plan for the day the grid, the weather, or the equipment lets you down.

Resiliency is the difference between an incident and an outage. We help operators find single points of failure, harden them, and rehearse the response before it's needed.

Redundancy & single-point-of-failure review

Which unit, breaker, pump, or sequence takes the site down if it fails — and what to do about it.

Backup power readiness

Generator, ATS, and UPS-adjacent mechanical coordination so cooling comes back when power does.

Temporary heating & cooling

Turnkey rental chillers, air handlers, and heat when a unit is down or lead times are long.

Emergency response plans

Site-specific playbooks: who to call, what to isolate, which spares are staged.

24/7 dispatch & critical spares

Staffed dispatch and on-call crews across the network, with spares for the parts that matter.

Seasonal & storm readiness

Pre-season startup, freeze protection, and post-event recovery across every site in the path.
